# Receipt Detective downloads

Receipt Detective finds the receipts for a home insurance claim in Gmail and Amazon (and photos of paper receipts),
reads them with AI on your own Mac, and makes the spreadsheet and PDFs for the adjuster.

**Download:** open [Releases](../../releases/latest) and get the `.dmg`. Open it, drag Receipt Detective to
Applications, and open it. The first launch sets everything up (including Ollama and the AI models). The app updates
itself from here.

This repository only holds the downloads.
